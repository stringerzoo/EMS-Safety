What makes it safer
Three trained crew members — pilot, nurse, and paramedic — each independently responsible for safety, and any one of them can call off a flight at any point.
Getting safer every year. The fatal accident rate has been cut nearly in half over two decades through systematic tracking of incidents and near-misses, not just accidents.
